Privacy Policy

Verona Ride · VERONA Technology Group LLC · Last updated: June 21, 2026

Summary: Verona Ride collects only the data necessary to provide a safe and reliable rideshare service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Who We Are

Verona Ride is a rideshare platform operated by VERONA Technology Group LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. Our service connects passengers with independent drivers across the United States.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@veronaride.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Date of birth (drivers only, encrypted)
  • Driver's license number (drivers only, encrypted)
  • Social Security Number partial (drivers only, encrypted, for 1099 tax reporting)

2.2 Location Data

  • Precise GPS location — collected while the app is in use to match passengers with nearby drivers and calculate routes
  • Background location (drivers only) — collected while a ride is in progress, even when the app is minimized, to keep passengers updated on the driver's position in real time. This data is not collected when there is no active ride.

2.3 Payment Information

Payment card details are processed exclusively by Stripe, Inc. and are never stored on Verona Ride servers. We only store transaction identifiers and amounts for billing records and 1099 tax reporting.

2.4 Trip Data

  • Pickup and drop-off addresses
  • Route taken during the trip
  • Trip duration and fare
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Driver notes after the trip

2.5 Device and Usage Data

  • Device type, operating system version
  • App version and crash reports (via Sentry)
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase)

2.6 Audio Recording (Drivers)

The driver app includes an optional audio recording feature for security purposes. Recordings are stored locally on the driver's device only and are never uploaded to Verona Ride servers. Drivers control this feature and can delete recordings at any time.

2.7 Identity Verification

  • Government-issued ID photo (drivers and passengers, when required)
  • Selfie photo for identity verification

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing the service — matching passengers with drivers, processing payments, calculating fares
  • Safety and security — verifying driver identity, SOS alerts, emergency contact notifications, route deviation detection
  • Communication — push notifications for ride status, receipts by email, scheduled ride reminders
  • Legal and tax compliance — 1099 tax forms for drivers, insurance audit logs required by TNC regulations
  • Service improvement — crash reports, performance monitoring
  • Fraud prevention — detecting and preventing unauthorized use

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Between Passengers and Drivers

During a ride, limited information is shared between the passenger and driver: first name, profile photo, vehicle description, and real-time location. Full contact details (phone number, email) are never shared directly.

4.2 Service Providers

  • Stripe, Inc. — payment processing
  • Google (Firebase) — authentication, push notifications, file storage
  • Google Maps Platform — maps, routing, geocoding
  • Supabase — database hosting (PostgreSQL on AWS infrastructure)
  • Railway — backend server hosting
  • Sentry — error and crash reporting (anonymized)

All service providers are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

4.4 We Do Not Sell Your Data

Verona Ride does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained while your account is active and for 3 years after deletion for legal and tax purposes
  • Trip records — retained for 7 years for tax and insurance compliance
  • GPS location logs — retained for 90 days for insurance audit purposes, then permanently deleted
  • Crash reports — retained for 30 days

6. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Sensitive personal data (SSN, date of birth, license number) is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies in the database ensure users can only access their own data
  • Failed login attempts trigger account lockout after 5 attempts

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out of marketing — unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@veronaride.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Verona Ride is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

To submit a CCPA request, email privacy@veronaride.com with the subject line "CCPA Request."

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
